# Third-party and supplied visual assets

Some application-specific navigation icons under
`app/src/main/assets/mi_market_icons/`, `app/src/main/assets/tiktok/`, and
`app/src/main/assets/weibo/` were prepared from the corresponding applications'
navigation UI for compatibility rendering. Their appearance, names, and marks
remain the property of their respective rights holders. They are not relicensed
under LiquidTab's GPL license. Before redistributing these files publicly, verify
that the applicable app terms and rights-holder permissions allow it. Removing
them may change the corresponding adapted app's icon rendering.

`app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/donation_qr.png` was supplied by the project
author for the in-app donation page. It is included at its original full image
bounds and rendered with fit/contain scaling; it is not cropped by the layout.
